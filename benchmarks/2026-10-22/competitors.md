**The completed default-configuration Rust #7 study shows no stable overall lead.** All six attempts are retained; pairs 1 and 3 are valid, while pair 2 is invalid because of a VM clock jump. Combined app-plus-PostgreSQL CPU per success is 2.905% higher overall across the two valid pairs, with inconsistent tails. The separate `WORKERS=2 ARTICLE_BATCH=1` configuration screen is **complete, not accepted**. Its valid pairs 1 and 2 reduce whole-target CPU per success by 5.0181% and 6.0277% with lower p95/p99, but worse p50. Pair 3's Goose arm is clock-invalid after approximately 926 seconds of wall-clock/monotonic drift. The required three valid, zero-error pairs were not obtained. Submitted defaults/source remain `3e908747`; the proposed promotion patch was not applied. The disclosed portable/corrected Goose #31 reference remains unmeasured; 26 active competitors have no performance comparison by us. Same-source ablation, conformance passes and single-core results do not establish first place or superiority over them.
