Bound PostgreSQL read admission so pending work cannot grow into an arbitrarily deep reader pipeline. Each reader now permits at most four outstanding Sync groups, with at most four SQL executions per group. Read queues preserve FIFO order, choose the oldest eligible work across families, and reconsider eligibility after each batch. Disconnected clients' unsent reads are removed; already-submitted work drains normally. Writers still return success after commit and obtain fresh article metadata by immutable ID.
