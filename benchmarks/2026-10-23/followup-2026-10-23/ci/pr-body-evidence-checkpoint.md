The optimization deduplicates compatible list/count work, shares metadata for actual selected feed articles, and batches feeds/comments without losing viewer flags, ordering or missing/empty behavior. Read pools wake after at most their configured collection window when eligible; writers collect for 1 ms before Sync and return success after commit. Bcrypt runs exactly the occupied lanes and ages collection from enqueue. Defaults are WORKERS=2, PG_READERS=3, HASHERS=2, ARTICLE_BATCH=1, READ_BATCH_US=5000 and WRITE_BATCH_US=1000.

Article updates and favorite/unfavorite responses reread committed metadata by immutable ID in a fresh statement snapshot, including concurrent slug renames. Legal embedded-NUL passwords retain all bytes and real cost-10 bcrypt work. Fresh JWT authentication runs on every authenticated request. There is no cross-request result cache, CPU affinity, schema/configuration change or worker restart; app and PostgreSQL share the challenge budget.
